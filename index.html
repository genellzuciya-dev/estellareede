The Prequel Novella

A Matter of A Piñón: The Baker's Missing Ledger

One missing bakery ledger, an inheritance compromised, and a mapmaker who can't resist connecting the dots.

Road-tripping cartographer Quinny Sinclair plans one night in Río Seco, a New Mexican town scented with sage and painted in fuchsia sunsets. Then her car breaks down, a black cat named Mr. Beans adopts her, and a grieving family's missing heirloom draws her in. She never turns back mid-trail.

Part recipe book, part shop ledger, part private diary, deceased baker Paz Delgado's handwritten book holds thirty years of recipes, business records, family history, and secrets about money that was never hers. Now she's gone, and so is the book. That's no accident.

Paz's niece swears it never existed. Her nephew insists it exists, and that it's his to inherit. Half the town wants it found. The rest couldn't care less, until Quinny starts asking questions.

She has two days until her car is fixed and the road pulls her home. Without the book, the bakery closes for good and an embezzler walks free.

A complete cozy mystery novella.

About the Author

Estella Reede

Estella Reede writes cozy mysteries rooted in the landscapes, kitchens, and small towns of New Mexico. The Quinny Sinclair Mysteries follow a cartographer who can't leave a map, or a mystery, alone.

She believes every good story starts with a trail someone left behind, and that no case should be solved on an empty stomach.
